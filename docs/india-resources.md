# India Support Resources (2026-09-05)

PTSD Coach ships US-only crisis numbers. India gets nothing. This doc is the
research base for Anchor's India-first "Find Support" directory. Every entry
verified with sources below; numbers must be re-verified before release.

## Offline display rules (app is offline-first)
- Always show phone numbers (all 8 helplines work by call, no internet).
- Therapist URL gets "offline unavailable" badge; pre-cache name + categories.
- Time-sensitive labels (iCall hours, Parivarthan 1–10pm) — not all lines are 24/7.
- Language tags per helpline. Priority: emergency → region match (Kashmir/Tamil Nadu) → Tele MANAS default → Vandrevala backup.

## Cost realities (design free-first)
- Govt OPD ₹10–50; private ₹800–2,500/session; budget online ₹499–800; trauma-specialized ₹1,799+. Almost no cashless insurance for therapy (2026). Always surface free helplines before paid options.

## Why India is the USP wedge
- Treatment gap 84–92%; flood-survivor PTSD pooled ~50% (12 studies); reported general-population rate suspiciously low (~0.24% — underdiagnosis/stigma masking).
- No India-made PTSD grounding app exists (Wysa/Amaha/Mentis cover mood/anxiety, not PTSD).
- Stigma vectors the design must respect: family honor (izzat) concealment, patriarchal gatekeeping, supernatural attributions, 22+ languages (English-only = exclusion), faith-first help-seeking, women/LGBTQ+ compound stigma.
- Design answers: discreet icon (doesn't say PTSD), no visible notifications, lock-screen safe, free-first ordering, trauma-type-agnostic framing, optional location-aware regional numbers (permission-gated), one-tap WhatsApp to Vandrevala when online, "for someone you trust" shareable text respecting family structure.
